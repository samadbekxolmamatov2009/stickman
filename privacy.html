Stickman Games — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Summary

Stickman Games does not collect, store or share any personal data.

Data used by the app

The app only saves game progress (high scores, unlocked levels, the sound setting). This data stays on your device and is never sent over the internet. It is removed when you uninstall the app.

What the app does not do

There is no account or sign-up, no advertising, no analytics, no in-app purchases and no third-party data collection. The app does not request access to the camera, microphone, location, contacts or files.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
