# Play Market sahifasi uchun matnlar

Ilova nomi (30 belgigacha): **Stickman Games: 15 ta o'yin**

Ilova identifikatori: `uz.samadbekxolmamatov.stickmangames` (yuklangandan keyin o'zgartirib bo'lmaydi)

## Qisqa tavsif (80 belgigacha)

**O'zbekcha:** 15 ta stickman o'yini bitta ilovada: jang, futbol, poyga, zombi va boshqalar.

**English:** 15 stickman games in one app: fight, football, race, zombies and more.

## To'liq tavsif

**O'zbekcha:**

Stickman Games — bitta ilovada 15 ta turli o'yin. Internet kerak emas, reklama yo'q.

🥊 Arena Fight — kombo, blok, qurol va olovli zarba
⚽ Stickman Futbol — bosh bilan ur, tep, super zarba
🏁 Stickman Race — marragacha poyga, 4 kishigacha
🏹 O'q-yoy dueli — shamol va tepalikni hisobga olib nishonga ol
🛡️ Minora mudofaasi — 4 xil himoyachi, 10 to'lqin va boss
🌉 Ko'prik qahramoni — ko'prik tashla, olmos yig'
🥷 Ninja kesish — mevalarni kes, bombaga tegma
💀 Battle Royale — oxirgi omon qolgan yutadi
🪢 Arqonda uchish — tebran va uch
🌋 Parkur minorasi — lavadan qochib tepaga chiq
🧟 Zombi himoyasi — to'lqinlardan omon qol, qobiliyat tanla
🏐 Voleybol — to'r orqali smesh ur
⚔️ Qilich dueli — kim tezroq bossa, o'sha yutadi
🔑 Qamoqdan qochish — qo'riqchilardan yashirin
🏍️ Moto tryuk — tepaliklarda salto qil

Do'stingiz bilan bitta telefonda o'ynang: Jang, Futbol, Voleybol, Kamon, Qilich dueli va Poyga 2 kishilik rejimni qo'llaydi.

**English:**

Stickman Games is a collection of 15 different games in one app. Works offline, no ads.

🥊 Arena Fight — combos, blocks, weapons and fireballs
⚽ Stickman Football — headers, kicks and super shots
🏁 Stickman Race — race to the finish, up to 4 players
🏹 Archery Duel — aim with wind and hills in mind
🛡️ Tower Defense — 4 defender classes, 10 waves and a boss
🌉 Bridge Hero — drop the bridge, grab the gems
🥷 Ninja Slice — slice the fruit, avoid the bombs
💀 Battle Royale — last one standing wins
🪢 Rope Swing — swing and fly
🌋 Parkour Tower — climb away from the lava
🧟 Zombie Defense — survive the waves, pick upgrades
🏐 Volleyball — spike it over the net
⚔️ Sword Duel — fastest reaction wins
🔑 Prison Escape — sneak past the guards
🏍️ Moto Tricks — flip over the hills

Play with a friend on one phone: Fight, Football, Volleyball, Archery, Sword Duel and Race support two players.
